Case study 01 · Encore ARC

Making complex operations work at scale.

A CRM transformation across Finance, Operations, Customer Service, and Legal teams.

FocusProduct execution
EnvironmentFinancial services
Reported outcomeMetric under verification

The challenge

Engineering capacity was limited, and each department had valid requests. I compared two factors: the manual hours that a CRM workflow could eliminate, and the consequences of leaving the process unchanged. I negotiated the sequence with stakeholders, focusing on customer-facing work and operational impact rather than simply who requested a feature first.

01 · EMI & NDC letters

Prioritized customer-facing letter generation so critical communications could be produced and sent to customers.

02 · Finance ledger workflows

Prioritized automation of labor-intensive customer ledger preparation to improve turnaround time.

03 · Customer Service tracker

Moved customer-service tracking ahead of remaining legal migration work to support smooth, efficient customer communication.

Deferred · Legal demand notice data

The Legal team could continue managing demand notice data in Excel temporarily while higher-impact workflows were built.

From process maps to production

The project replaced fragmented spreadsheet processes with CRM-supported workflows. A reported turnaround-time improvement of three days to 24 hours is being verified against the specific process before being presented as a public metric.

Simplifying pricing and catalogue management in a traditional family business.

The problem

A large catalogue and changing raw-material prices made manual product lookup and pricing harder to maintain.

Discovery

The user was my father. Instead of assuming what a good interface should look like, I focused on how he actually worked and what made the tool easier for him to use.

What I built

I developed a low-code pricing and catalogue tool in Bubble.io and iterated on the workflow and interface.
